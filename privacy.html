隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 23 日

「注音小達人」（以下稱「本 App」）是給 3 到 8 歲兒童使用的學習 App。本 App 不蒐集、不傳送、也不販售任何個人資料。

我們蒐集哪些資料

沒有。本 App：

  • 不需要註冊帳號，也不用登入。
  • 沒有廣告。
  • 沒有使用分析、追蹤工具或任何第三方 SDK。
  • 不會要求姓名、生日、電子郵件、位置、照片、聯絡人，也不使用麥克風和相機。

只存在裝置上的資料

下面這些資料只儲存在你的裝置上，不會傳送給開發者或任何第三方，開發者也無法取得：

  • 設定：出題範圍、每關題數、選項數量、聲音等。
  • 練習紀錄：日期、級別、作答題數、答對與答錯的題數。
  • 免費試玩的使用量，以及這台裝置是否已經解鎖完整版。試玩使用量存放在裝置的鑰匙圈，重新安裝 App 後仍會保留。

手寫題的筆跡只在裝置上即時辨識，不會上傳。題目的語音是 App 內建的音檔和裝置內建的語音功能，在裝置上播放。

刪除 App 會一併刪除設定與練習紀錄。

購買

完整版的購買、回復購買與兌換代碼都由 Apple 的 App Store 處理。開發者不會收到你的姓名、付款方式或 Apple 帳號資料；本 App 只會從 App Store 得知目前的 Apple 帳號是否擁有完整版。Apple 如何處理你的資料，請參考 Apple 的隱私權政策。

網路連線

學習內容不需要連網。本 App 只有在向 App Store 查詢價格、購買、回復購買或兌換代碼時才會連線。

兒童隱私

本 App 的對象是兒童。因為本 App 不蒐集任何資料，所以也不會蒐集兒童的個人資料。設定、練習紀錄與購買頁面都需要先通過家長驗證才能進入。

政策變更

這份政策如果有變更，會更新這個頁面和上面的日期。

聯絡方式

對這份政策有任何問題，請來信：joseyarashio@icloud.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Zhuyin Master (注音小達人, “the app”) is a learning app for children aged 3 to 8. The app does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The app:

  • has no account system and no sign-in;
  • has no advertising;
  • has no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs;
  • never asks for a name, birthday, email address, location, photos or contacts, and does not use the microphone or camera.

Data stored only on your device

The following stays on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to any third party, and the developer cannot access it:

  • Settings (question scope, questions per round, number of choices, sound).
  • Practice records (date, level, number of questions answered, correct and incorrect counts).
  • Free-trial usage and whether the full version is unlocked on this device. Trial usage is kept in the device keychain and remains after the app is reinstalled.

Handwriting is recognized on the device in real time and is never uploaded. Speech comes from audio files bundled with the app and from the device’s built-in speech feature, and is played on the device.

Deleting the app deletes its settings and practice records.

Purchases

Purchasing the full version, restoring a purchase and redeeming a code are all handled by Apple’s App Store. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information; the app only learns from the App Store whether the current Apple Account owns the full version. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for how Apple handles your data.

Network access

The learning content works offline. The app connects to the network only to ask the App Store for the price, to purchase, to restore a purchase or to redeem a code.

Children’s privacy

The app is made for children. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no personal data from children. Settings, practice records and the purchase screen are behind a parental gate.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
